What the work includes

Each piece can be bought on its own, or combined into a single programme.

Use-case discovery

We list candidate uses of AI across your operation and score each on value, feasibility and risk.

Data readiness

We check whether your data is accurate, accessible and sufficient, and fix gaps before models are built.

Pilot to production

We build a focused pilot, measure it against agreed targets, then deploy it into daily work.

Governance and security

We set rules for data access, model monitoring and responsible use, and protect AI systems from misuse and attack.

How it runs

Three stages, each ending with something you can use.

  1. Explore

    We shortlist use cases and agree what success looks like.

  2. Prove

    We run a time-boxed pilot on real data and report the results honestly.

  3. Scale

    We deploy the winners with monitoring, training and support.
